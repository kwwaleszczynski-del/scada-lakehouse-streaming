Streaming SCADA telemetry, late-arriving backfill and Medallion lakehouse

Event-driven wind, solar PV and PCC revenue meter pipeline across a 50 MW hybrid plant (10 × 3.0 MW wind turbines, 4 × 5.0 MW PV inverters, 2 PCC revenue meters): quantifying how naive ingestion-time windowing fails during cellular LTE router reconnect bursts, how fold-aware UTC normalization resolves 23 h / 25 h Central European (Europe/Warsaw) DST transitions, and how automated Silver DQ rules feed 15-minute Parquet and TimescaleDB continuous aggregates. Code and SQL: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/scada-lakehouse-streaming.

2. Central European DST transition (25 October 2026, 25-hour fall-back day)

Legacy field loggers exporting local Europe/Warsaw CSV files repeat 02:00:00..02:59:00 twice during the October fall-back transition (first in CEST UTC+2, then in CET UTC+1). Naive local-key deduplication overwrites 02A CEST with 02B CET, losing 1 full hour of generation (-28.03 MWh, -6.02 % of daily fleet energy). Deterministic disambiguation via monotonic kWh_total register continuity assigns fold=0/1 and recovers all 100 quarter-hours (0.000 MWh error).
