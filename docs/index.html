Streaming SCADA telemetry, late-arriving backfill and Medallion lakehouse

Event-driven wind, solar PV and PCC revenue meter pipeline across a 50 MW hybrid plant (10 × 3.0 MW wind turbines, 4 × 5.0 MW PV inverters, 2 PCC revenue meters): quantifying how naive ingestion-time windowing fails during cellular LTE router reconnect bursts, how fold-aware UTC normalization resolves 23 h / 25 h Central European (Europe/Warsaw) DST transitions, and how rule-based Silver DQ checks feed 15-minute Gold Parquet tables (with TimescaleDB continuous-aggregate DDL generated alongside). Code and SQL: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/scada-lakehouse-streaming.

Scope: every number on this page is computed by the repository's pipeline from a seeded, synthetic 7-day fleet simulation. No MQTT/Kafka broker and no database run here; broker arrival times are simulated, and the TimescaleDB DDL is generated as text but not executed.

1. Interactive LTE router outage & event-time watermark simulator

Select a simulated LTE router outage and a streaming watermark horizon W. During an outage, 1-minute PLC packets buffer locally and flush in a burst on reconnect. Grouping by broker arrival time (ingested_at) leaves the outage quarter-hours empty and piles the backlog into the reconnect quarter-hour: across the simulated outages that bucket carries × its true energy (median; range ). Event-time bucketing with W = 6 h and an idempotent ON CONFLICT (asset_id, timestamp_utc) DO UPDATE places % of delayed intervals in their own quarter-hour and counts % duplicate energy.

2. Central European DST transition (25 October 2026, 25-hour fall-back day)

Legacy field loggers exporting local Europe/Warsaw CSV files repeat 02:00:00..02:59:00 twice during the October fall-back transition (first in CEST UTC+2, then in CET UTC+1). Naive local-key deduplication overwrites 02A CEST with 02B CET and loses one hour of generation (- MWh, - % of the day's fleet energy). Assigning fold=0/1 by occurrence order within the monotonic kWh_total register restores all quarter-hours, with % of rows mapped back to their true UTC timestamp.

3. Automated Bronze → Silver SCADA sensor QC & counter rollover recovery

Inspect how the Silver DQ engine isolates and imputes frozen ultrasonic anemometers (FLATLINE, rolling 30-min std == 0), pyranometer EMI surges & nighttime offsets (SPIKE_OR_BOUNDS), and cumulative uint32 energy counter rollovers at 4,294,967.296 kWh (COUNTER_RESET): macro-F1 across 1-minute asset-intervals. The faults are injected by the same simulator the rules were written for, so this is a consistency check, not a field accuracy estimate; every miss is an onset minute of an injected window (see the README).
